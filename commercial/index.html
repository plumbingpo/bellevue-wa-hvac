Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills dump enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, anywhere in Bellevue and the surrounding area.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every Bellevue HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes with MERV ratings matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
